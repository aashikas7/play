Main quest · Fashion: D2C

Savana

How a digital-first fashion brand uses trend velocity, social commerce, and a mobile-first shop to win Gen Z's attention in India's D2C market.

Why Savana

Savana is where fashion retail in India is heading: a digital-first, Gen Z brand with a strong presence at home and a read on international trends. Design, pricing psychology, and loyalty have to work as one system if growth is going to last.

  • Trend velocity: rapid design-to-launch cycles
  • Social commerce: influencer-led discovery
  • Mobile-first shopping: an app-centric experience

What makes it a strong product

Trend velocity

Rapid design-to-launch, with a catalogue that mirrors Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest in near real time. It feels current, not seasonal.

Mobile-first

Discovery, wishlist, checkout, and payment live in the app. Push notifications cover drops, offers, and restocks.

Coupons as product

A coupon is waiting almost every time the app opens. Predictable discounts lower checkout hesitation and create a small win before the purchase.

Savana Club

The loyalty engine: Explorer, Enthusiast, Insider, then Icon. Icon perks include shopping rewards, member pricing, birthday surprises, and VIP service.

What I would build next

Standard delivery is about 21 days, which breaks the impulse that fashion depends on. The catalogue is also still focused on women.

1: 60-minute express

Metro-first delivery for high-demand SKUs, through dark stores.

2: Buy the Look

One tap to purchase a full influencer-curated outfit.

3: Men's fashion

Capsule drops first: streetwear, casual, party. Test demand with influencers before a full expansion.

Takeaway

Savana works because it solves global fashion access for budget-conscious Indian shoppers, it understands Gen Z, and it joins trends, pricing psychology, loyalty, and technology.
